## AI Ethics & Responsible AI

### Overview

Responsible AI is the practice of designing, developing, and deploying artificial intelligence systems with the intention of empowering employees and businesses, and fairly impacting customers and society. This allows companies to build trust and scale AI with confidence. It's a framework that guides AI development to be fair, transparent, accountable, secure, and respectful of user privacy. As data scientists and machine learning engineers, it's our responsibility to understand these principles and actively work to mitigate bias, ensure our models are explainable, and consider the real-world impact of our work.

### Projects & Exercises

1.  **Conduct a Bias Audit:**
    *   Choose a well-known dataset like the "Adult" census dataset or the "COMPAS" recidivism dataset.
    *   Train a simple classification model (e.g., logistic regression).
    *   Analyze the model's performance across different demographic groups (e.g., race, gender). Document any disparities in error rates and discuss potential sources of bias.

2.  **Implement Model Explainability:**
    *   Train a model (e.g., a gradient boosting model on a housing price dataset).
    *   Use a library like SHAP or LIME to explain individual predictions.
    *   Write a short report explaining which features were most influential for a few specific predictions, as if you were explaining the model's decision to a non-technical stakeholder.

3.  **Ethical Framework Case Study:**
    *   Select a real-world AI application (e.g., a social media recommendation algorithm, a generative AI art tool, or an automated hiring tool).
    *   Evaluate it against the principles outlined in Microsoft's or Google's Responsible AI framework.
    *   Identify potential ethical risks and suggest mitigation strategies.
