### Generative AI & Foundation Models

#### Overview

Generative AI is a transformative field of artificial intelligence focused on creating new, original content—from text and images to code and music. At the core of many modern generative systems are **Foundation Models**. These are large-scale models, like Large Language Models (LLMs), trained on vast amounts of data. They serve as a powerful base that can be adapted for a wide range of specific tasks, such as summarization, translation, and question-answering. Understanding foundation models is key to grasping how today's most advanced AI tools work and how you can leverage them in your own data science projects.

#### Projects & Exercises

1.  **Prompt Engineering Practice**: Use a publicly available LLM-based chatbot or image generator. Your goal is to create a specific output by carefully crafting your prompts. Try to generate a short story in the style of a specific author or an image that combines two unrelated concepts. Document how changes in your prompt affect the model's output.
2.  **Explore a Model's Architecture**: Clone the [Foundation Models Implementation](https://github.com/Arenaa/foundation-models-implementation) repository. Choose one model (like a simple Vision Transformer or GAN) and read through the code. Add comments to the code explaining what each block does. You don't need to run it—the goal is to understand the structure and flow of data.
3.  **Summarization Task**: Find a long article or a research paper. Use a pre-trained summarization model (e.g., from the Hugging Face Hub) to generate a summary. Compare the AI-generated summary to the original abstract or your own summary. Evaluate its accuracy and coherence.
