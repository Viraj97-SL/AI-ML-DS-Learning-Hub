### Generative AI & Foundation Models

#### Overview

Generative AI is a branch of artificial intelligence focused on creating new, original content—from text and images to code and music. The engine powering many of these powerful applications is a **foundation model**. These are large-scale models, like GPT-4 (text), DALL-E (images), or Whisper (speech-to-text), trained on vast amounts of data. Because of their broad training, a single foundation model can be adapted to perform a wide range of tasks, such as writing an email, debugging code, or translating languages, making them a versatile cornerstone of modern AI development.

#### Projects & Exercises

Get hands-on experience by interacting with publicly available models.

1.  **Model Comparison**:
    *   Choose a question or a task, for example: "Explain the concept of photosynthesis to a 10-year-old in three paragraphs."
    *   Submit this exact prompt to at least two different publicly available LLMs (like ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Claude, etc.).
    *   Compare the outputs. Note the differences in tone, structure, accuracy, and creativity.

2.  **Iterative Prompting for Image Generation**:
    *   Use a free image generation tool (like Microsoft Designer or Adobe Firefly).
    *   Start with a simple prompt, e.g., `a cat sitting on a chair`.
    *   Iteratively add more detail to your prompt to change the output. Try adding:
        *   **Style**: `...in the style of a watercolor painting.`
        *   **Setting**: `...in a cozy library with a fireplace.`
        *   **Lighting**: `...with warm, afternoon sunlight streaming through a window.`
    *   Observe how each new detail influences the final image.

3.  **Summarization and Format Conversion**:
    *   Find a news article that is 4-5 paragraphs long.
    *   Ask a generative AI tool to summarize the article in a single paragraph.
    *   In a new prompt, ask it to summarize the same article as a list of three bullet points.
    *   Evaluate the quality of both summaries. Did the model capture the key information accurately in each format?
